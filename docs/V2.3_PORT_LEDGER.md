# v2.3.0 port ledger

Inventory taken 2026-10-22 for the fork's 2.3.0 release. It records every
upstream commit, third-party fork and upstream issue/PR that was considered,
what it is worth to this fork, and what was decided. Status values:
**ported** (cherry-picked with `-x` or a hunk port), **rewritten** (same intent,
fork code), **skipped** (with the reason), **deferred** (worth doing, not in
2.3.0).

Baseline: `origin/main` at `8294ff1` (2.2.10 plus PR #80). Upstream:
`upstream/main` at `9174751` (ProxyShard v2.1.0, released 2026-10-22).
Merge base `39745d7`. The fork squash-merged upstream through v2.0.1
(`187b8bd`) in `03b9ffc` (#37), so the first nine upstream commits below are
content-present even though `git cherry` reports them as missing.

### Vendored, generated and binary content

Not counted as source and not ported on its own: `ui-kit/**` build output and
tokens, `e2e/**` (≈200 specs for the redesigned UI), every lockfile, 
`src-tauri/gen/schemas/*`, icons (`png`/`icns`/`ico`), `docs/automation-module-example/**`,
the upstream patch log, `src/shared/i18n/locales/{zh,ru}.json`, and the
Chromium/Widevine/fingerprint archives, which are fetched from the CDN by hash
through `runtime.json` and never stored in Git.
